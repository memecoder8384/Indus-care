Our Story & Purpose

About Indus Care Foundation — Healthcare NGO in Hapur

Since 2020, Indus Care Foundation has operated 24/7 charitable blood banks and healthcare drives across Hapur, Gulaothi, and Hasanpur to ensure emergency blood support is accessible to all.

Founded on Compassion & Action

Indus Care Foundation was established in 2020 by a collective of doctors, social activists, and corporate leaders who identified a alarming shortfall in blood availability and basic diagnostic infrastructure in underserved Indian districts.

What started as a single voluntary blood collection drive has expanded into three fully operational charitable blood centres, hundreds of medical camps, and direct life-saving support for over 150,000 beneficiaries.

Our Growth

Journey & Milestones

2020

Foundation Establishment

Registered as a non-profit foundation during peak medical urgency. Conducted first 10 corporate blood donation drives.

2021

First Charitable Blood Centre

Launched Hapur Charitable Blood Centre, ensuring round-the-clock blood component separation and emergency supply.

2022

Expansion to 3 Centres

Opened Vardan and Indus Charitable Blood Centres. Reached over 10,000 units of blood collected annually.

2023 - Present

450+ Health Camps & 100+ CSR Partners

Scaled nationwide outreach through corporate CSR tie-ups, diagnostic camps, and free medicine distribution.
