Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about finding a blood bank in Hapur, emergency blood services, and donation procedures.

help Where is the nearest blood bank near me in Hapur?

Indus Care Foundation operates a 24/7 charitable blood bank & blood center in Hapur, serving patients across Hapur, Gulaothi, and Hasanpur. You can reach our emergency helpline at +91 63989 21025 or +91 79007 45068 anytime for immediate blood support.

help What blood components are available at Indus Care Blood Center in Hapur?

Our charitable blood center provides Whole Human Blood, Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBC), Single Donor Platelets (SDP Apheresis Jumbo Packs), Platelet Concentrates, and Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP).

help Are emergency blood services available 24/7 in Hapur, Gulaothi & Hasanpur?

Yes, our emergency blood services and blood donation centres operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across Hapur, Gulaothi (Bulandshahr), and Hasanpur (Amroha).
