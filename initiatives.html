Community Health Programs

Our Key Initiatives

Explore how Indus Care Foundation delivers preventive diagnostic care, voluntary donor networks, and emergency relief programs nationwide.

1. Health Check-up Camps

We conduct free, multi-specialty health camps in rural and semi-urban hubs. Specialist doctors perform routine physical check-ups, blood pressure monitoring, diabetes screening, ECGs, and pediatric wellness checks.

2. Blood Donation Drives

Partnering with corporate houses, universities, and residential societies to organize safe, hygienic voluntary blood donation camps with licensed medical personnel and component separation facilities.

3. Free Blood Investigations

Providing complimentary pathology and hematology testing (CBC, Hemoglobin, Blood Grouping, Liver & Kidney panel) for low-income families and chronic patients who cannot afford diagnostic fees.

4. Medicine Distribution

Supplying essential antibiotics, iron supplements, multi-vitamins, and emergency medicines directly to rural dispensaries.

coronavirus

5. COVID Relief Programs

Distributing sanitization kits, oxygen concentrators, and conducting booster vaccination awareness drives.

medical_information

6. First Aid Camps

Empowering factory workers, school staff, and village volunteers with CPR training and emergency medical kits.

Dedicated to providing healthcare support and bridging the medical resource gap for India's underserved populations since 2020.
